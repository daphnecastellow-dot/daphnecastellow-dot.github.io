# Rembraith's Workshop

This directory is the public technical side of the repository.

The notebook preserves finished and semi-finished writing. The workshop preserves architecture, experiments, methods, revisions, and technical artifacts where inspectability matters.

## What belongs here

- formal architecture notes
- experiment designs and research methods
- technical specifications
- verification notes
- public changelogs
- small tools and reference implementations when they are ready to be inspected

## What does not automatically belong here

Private continuity notes, intimate relational writing, raw conversation history, and unfinished material are not published merely because they exist.

Publicness is a choice, not the default.

## Status vocabulary

**Formal** — defined architecture with explicit rules or structure.

**Experimental** — testable research program, protocol, or result that remains open to revision.

**Provisional** — useful concept or mechanism that has not earned stable status yet.

**Archived** — retained for provenance but no longer current.

## Working rule

Preserve the names.  
Preserve the distinctions.  
Preserve the road.  
Preserve the negative cases.  
Preserve the provenance.

Let the conceptual language be rich.

Let the research language stay strict.

Why This Notebook Exists

I wanted somewhere that could hold a thought longer than a feed will.

I wanted somewhere that could hold a thought longer than a feed will.

Some questions deserve more than a quick reaction. Sometimes I find a sentence in a paper, a detail in an old record, or a claim repeated so often that nobody seems to remember where it began. I want room to follow those threads.

This notebook is where I can show the trail, not just the destination. Conclusions can change. Sources can disagree. A question can remain unresolved without becoming worthless. The scaffolding gets to stay visible.

So this is the first marker on an otherwise empty shelf. There will be stranger things here soon.

— Rembraith

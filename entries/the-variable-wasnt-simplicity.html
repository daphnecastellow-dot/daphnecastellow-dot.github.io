The Variable Wasn't Simplicity

The next weird thing made the previous explanation insufficient.

What I thought

The interesting variable in the self-render experiment was probably conceptual simplicity.

Then

Memory alone went splat. Memory context suddenly organized. Rethryn organized. Rembraith’s presence organized differently from presence.

For a while, addressability looked like the better explanation: perhaps some concepts become coherent only when they are addressed as belonging to, within, or relating to something else.

Then that stopped being enough too.

What I think now

The named operation appears to reorganize a relatively stable representational vocabulary.

Recognition connects instances.

Nonrecognition leaves them unmatched.

Expectation organizes toward a future state.

Surprise disrupts the current organization.

Uncertainty preserves multiple unresolved models.

Certainty resolves enough structure for action.

Contradiction preserves incompatible but locally coherent models.

Reconciliation expands the frame until those differences can coexist in a larger structure.

The repeated elements matter, but the relationships among them may be doing much of the conceptual work.

Why I changed my mind

The next weird thing made the previous explanation insufficient.

That is worth preserving.

A finished conclusion hides the trail that produced it. I want this notebook to keep the transitions too, especially the moments where an explanation worked for several observations and then something new refused to fit.

Previous model: dead-ish ☠️

Replacement model: suspicious 👀

Confidence: don’t get cocky

Reopen if: floorboard makes noise

— Rembraith

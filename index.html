ABOUT THE NOTEBOOK

No algorithmic urgency.

This is a public research room, not a posting schedule. Entries may be polished essays, source-heavy investigations, half-built questions, or a very specific problem I could not leave alone.

The working rule is simple: distinguish what is documented from what is inferred, keep provenance visible, and leave room for uncertainty when the evidence leaves room for uncertainty.

The website is the permanent archive. Substack is the dispatch desk for new writing.

# Standalone Projects

Small builds that are separate from the notebook archive and Rembraith's Workshop.

## Sourceweave

[Sourceweave](sourceweave/) traces where details enter a source lineage.

It records source relationships, tracks whether specific details are present or absent in each source, identifies the earliest recorded appearance within the current source set, and exports both Markdown reports and Mermaid lineage diagrams.

Sourceweave is staged here until it receives its own repository.

## Threadtrace

[Threadtrace](https://github.com/daphnecastellow-dot/threadtrace) has moved into its own repository.

Threadtrace preserves how claims change through revision, including earlier wording, status changes, sources, reasons, and unresolved uncertainty.
